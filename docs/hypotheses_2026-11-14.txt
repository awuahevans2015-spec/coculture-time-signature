H1 Culture time: pericyte-endothelial coupling and basement membrane genes rise with culture day.
H2 Crosslinking density (a proxy for matrix stiffness; direction not pre-specified): adhesion and mechanotransduction genes (integrins, YAP/TAZ targets) change with crosslinking percentage.

H4 Part of the signal is within-cell regulation, not only the endothelial:pericyte ratio.
